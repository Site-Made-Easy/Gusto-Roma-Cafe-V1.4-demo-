Made Fresh Daily

Every morning our dough is kneaded by hand and our sauces are simmered slowly with San Marzano tomatoes, olive oil and basil. No shortcuts, no compromises — just the food we grew up loving, served warm on Law College Road.

  • Hand-rolled pasta, made each morning
  • Slow-simmered sauces, never from a jar
  • Imported olive oil & Italian cheeses
